Delete your account

You can permanently delete your LASTSEEN account from inside the app, including a guest account.

  1. Open Settings → Delete account.
  2. Select I understand this is permanent.
  3. Tap Delete account. Keep your phone connected until you see “Account deleted”.

What is removed immediately?

Your account, nickname, avatar colour, linked email address and push notification token are removed. You leave any running game and location sharing stops. This cannot be undone.

What happens to past games?

Your name in other players’ match history becomes “Deleted player”, and those records are no longer linked to your account. Games, including ping snapshots and match results, are retained for no more than 90 days, then deleted. Deleting your account does not erase your friends’ match history immediately.

Technical server logs follow the hosting provider’s retention, usually a few days. Read the full privacy policy for all retention periods.

No longer have the app?

Request deletion by emailing dominik@fersion.at. Include your nickname and, if you linked one, write from that email address. For a guest account, include enough match details to help identify it. We may need to verify that the account is yours before deleting it. Never send a password.

Reset or delete?

Reset guest identity signs you out on this phone. Server data stays until the 90-day cleanup. Choose Delete account to permanently remove the account everywhere.
