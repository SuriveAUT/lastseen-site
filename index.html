How a match works

  1. Gather your people. Pick a nickname, create a game and share the join code. No email required.
  2. Set the ground rules. Choose a safe play area, hiding time and ping interval. Pick Hiders or Seekers, allow location access and ready up.
  3. Give Hiders a head start. Meet at the same starting point. The host starts the countdown; Hiders leave while Seekers wait.
  4. Follow the clues. Seekers hunt using timed pings. Teammates see each other live; a Hider’s ping stays where it was recorded.
  5. Find them. Confirm it. Get close and request a capture, or use the Hider’s capture code. Seekers win by catching everyone; Hiders win if any remain when the time limit runs out.

Play in public places, respect boundaries and traffic, and stay aware of your surroundings.

Support

A few things to know before your next match.

Why does location permission say “Always”?

It lets your position update during a running match when you lock your phone or switch apps. Only your teammates see your live position; opponents see ping snapshots. Tracking stops when you leave, are captured or the match ends. Outside matches, location is only read if you tap “Use my location” to choose a game zone. You can change permission in your phone’s settings.

Why pings instead of live opponent positions?

A ping is a clue: where a Hider was at one moment. It never follows them. Between pings, Seekers have to predict the next move. Hiders’ live positions are shared only with their own team.

We found each other, but GPS is wrong. How do we capture?

Use the capture code on the Hider’s phone. The Seeker can enter it or scan its QR code to confirm the capture without relying on GPS proximity. Codes expire, so use the one currently displayed. Both phones still need a connection.

How do I leave a game or stop sharing in an emergency?

Open the game menu, choose Emergency leave and confirm. Tracking stops on your phone even offline; reconnect and retry the leave request so the game can tell your teammates you have left. You can use this whenever you need to leave a running game.

How much battery does a match use?

GPS, mobile data and keeping the map on screen use battery. LASTSEEN reduces update frequency in the background and stops tracking when you leave, are captured or the match ends. Start with a charged phone, dim the screen and lock it between checks. Battery use varies with your phone, reception and match length.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete account, confirm that you understand it is permanent, then tap Delete account. You need an internet connection. Reset guest identity only signs you out on this phone. See account deletion details for what is removed and how to request deletion by email.

What happens if the host disconnects?

The match keeps running. Once the host has been offline for about 90 seconds, another active player can choose Become host to manage pauses, captures and the end of the game. A brief disconnect does not reset the match.

Which phones are supported?

LASTSEEN supports iOS 16.4 or later and Android 8 or later. Each player needs a phone with location services and an internet connection. Allow background location for reliable play with the screen locked.
